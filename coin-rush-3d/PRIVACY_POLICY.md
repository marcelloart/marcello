# Privacy Policy — Coin Rush 3D

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

Coin Rush 3D is an Android mobile game published under the package name `com.marcelloart.coinrush3d`.

## 1. Information stored by Coin Rush 3D

Coin Rush 3D stores gameplay-related information locally on the user's device, including:

- game progress and unlocked levels;
- checkpoint progress;
- coins, stars, inventory and power-ups;
- achievements and mission progress;
- daily reward state;
- cosmetic/outfit selections;
- audio, vibration and graphics settings.

Coin Rush 3D version 1.0.0 does not require a player account and does not use a custom cloud backend to store these gameplay records.

Local gameplay data remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

## 2. Advertising

Coin Rush 3D uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are shown when the player voluntarily selects an ad-based reward, such as continuing gameplay after losing lives or receiving additional game time.

Google and its advertising partners may process information including, where applicable:

- IP address and approximate/general location inferred from IP;
- device and advertising identifiers;
- app interactions such as launches, taps and video views;
- diagnostics and performance information;
- advertising-related information used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

Google's own privacy policies and data practices apply to data processed by Google AdMob.

## 3. Consent and advertising privacy

Where required, Coin Rush 3D uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent before production ads are requested.

A privacy-options entry point is available in the production version of the app when applicable.

## 4. Data sharing

Coin Rush 3D does not operate its own system for selling personal information.

Data handled by Google AdMob may be shared or processed by Google and advertising partners according to Google's policies and the user's consent choices where applicable.

## 5. Data security

Gameplay information stored directly by Coin Rush 3D remains in local application storage on the device.

Advertising-related network communication is handled by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and uses the security mechanisms provided by those services.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data is retained on the device until the user clears Coin Rush 3D application data or uninstalls the game.

Coin Rush 3D does not currently provide user accounts, so there is no server-side Coin Rush 3D account database to delete.

Retention of data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's applicable policies and settings.

## 7. Children and target audience

Coin Rush 3D does not provide a custom account system for collecting a player's name, email address or profile information.

Advertising configuration, age-related treatment and Google Play target-audience declarations are managed according to the app's final Play Store distribution settings and applicable Google policies.

## 8. Third-party services

The primary third-party advertising service integrated in Coin Rush 3D version 1.0.0 is:

- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
